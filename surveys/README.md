## Model-based Reinforcement Learning: A Survey

**Core · 2023 · survey**

Thomas M. Moerland; Joost Broekens; Aske Plaat; Catholijn M. Jonker. Foundations and Trends in Machine Learning 2023.

The conceptual reference for model learning and planning–learning integration.

**Read for:** Taxonomy, partial observability, uncertainty, and model usage; preprint originally posted 2020.

[Open resource](https://arxiv.org/abs/2006.16712)

## A survey of world models for physical AI with uncertainty representation and control

**Core · 2026 · survey**

Sven Kirchner; Nils Purschke; Alois Knoll. Discover Artificial Intelligence 2026; journal review.

A recent control-centered map emphasizing optimization, uncertainty, and closed-loop evaluation.

**Read for:** Planner exploitation, horizon management, state abstraction and calibration; physical examples are secondary.

[Open resource](https://link.springer.com/article/10.1007/s44163-026-02122-1)
